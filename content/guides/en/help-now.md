A meltdown or shutdown is not bad behaviour. Your child is overwhelmed. The goal right now is safety and less input, not teaching.

## 1. Keep everyone safe

- Move things that could hurt away. Move other children away.
- Stay close enough to help, far enough to give space.

## 2. Turn everything down

- Fewer words. A soft, slow voice, or no words at all.
- Lower lights and noise. Move to a quieter place if your child can walk there.

## 3. Offer what usually calms

- Offer, don't force: a favourite object, deep pressure, a blanket, water.
- Wait. It may take a while to pass.

## 4. Afterwards

- Rest first. Talk later, if at all.
- Think about what came just before: noise, hunger, pain, tiredness, a change of plan.

## Get help now if

- Your child is hurt, has trouble breathing, or you cannot keep them safe: call your emergency number.
