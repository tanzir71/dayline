# Product

## In one line

Dayline is a day record for families of autistic children, kept on the parent's phone: what happened, what changed, what helps. It gives whoever is with the child a brief, gives each doctor a report, and helps from day one even if nobody logs anything.

## Who it is for

| Person | Needs | Gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parent (owner of the phone) | One place for meds, sleep, gut, hard moments, changes; less to remember | Today, Help now, guides, reminders, visit reports |
| Grandparent, sitter, other parent | Know what's going on right now | The brief: shown on the parent's phone in show mode, or as a PDF on WhatsApp |
| Teacher | Know the child on day one | The brief or passport as a PDF; tells the parent how the day went, and the parent notes it |
| Doctor, therapist | The full picture in two minutes, including other prescribers' changes | A two-page PDF on WhatsApp, or the report shown on screen |

First market: Bangladesh (Android-first, Bangla and English, WhatsApp for sharing, slow networks). Design so it works anywhere.

## Principles

1. **Useful before the first log.** Guides, Help now, the child profile, reminders and visit prep work with zero data.
2. **Logging is a side effect.** Dose taps, ending Help now, the weekly check-in and Mark given in show mode create the record.
3. **Changes, not charts.** Every medicine or routine change gets a review date and an honest before-and-after.
4. **Show the evidence.** Counts, not claims. Nothing shown below the data threshold.
5. **Brief whoever is next.** The brief is generated, never typed. Show the phone or send a PDF.
6. **Nothing leaves the phone by itself.** No accounts, no server, no cloud. Data leaves only as a PDF, a print or a backup file the parent chooses to send.
7. **Strengths, not deficits.** Wins and "what helps" are as visible as hard moments.
8. **No guilt.** No streaks, no "you haven't logged". Welcome back after gaps with three yes/no questions.

## The five workflows (as on the landing page)

1. **Daily loop:** tap once (or just mark the dose) → the day line fills itself → show the next caregiver the brief, or send it as a PDF.
2. **A hard moment:** open Help now → try what works for this child → tap "It's over"; it is saved with what helped.
3. **A change:** note a new medicine, dose or routine → two weeks later see before and after, overlaps flagged → keep it or add it to the next visit.
4. **A doctor visit:** prep in 60 seconds → one two-page report per doctor, sent to their WhatsApp → every prescriber's changes on one page.
5. **Coming back after weeks:** no streaks or guilt → three yes/no questions → back to today.

## Nudges (the only ones allowed)

Install Dayline (mobile, first visit, at most twice) · dose due · tablets running low · visit in 3 days ("prep in 60 seconds") · routine change coming from the calendar (Ramadan, Eid, school holidays, exams) with the matching guide · a change review is due · monthly backup. Never "you haven't logged".

## Voice and copy

- Say: child, hard moment (or the parent's own word), win, what helps, change, "seen 6 of 8 times".
- Avoid: patient, episode, non-compliant, "X causes Y", alarm red, exclamation marks.
- Plain, calm, short sentences. Address the parent as "you". Use the child's name.
- Every empty state says what will appear and offers one useful action now.
- Medical boundary line, used where relevant: "Dayline never suggests doses or changes; ask the prescriber."

## Safety

- Not a medical device; no diagnosis, no dosing, no treatment recommendations.
- Help now includes call buttons (parent's contacts; emergency number, 999 in Bangladesh, configurable).
- Guides: reviewer, date, sources on every page; drafts carry a visible banner; no unproven or harmful "treatments".
- Data lives only on the phone: remind about backups, and say plainly that clearing the browser or losing the phone loses data without one.
